Delivery is git-sync from {{ .Values.rules.gitSync.repo }} at {{ .Values.rules.gitSync.ref }},
every {{ .Values.rules.gitSync.period }}. The chain is merge, sync, symlink flip,

reload after it lands.
{{- else }}
Delivery is the ConfigMap {{ .Values.rules.configMap.name }}. A ConfigMap mount has no
exec hook, so nothing posts to /-/reload on its own: add a reloader through
extraContainers, or send SIGHUP yourself.
{{- end }}
